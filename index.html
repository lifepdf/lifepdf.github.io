PRIVACY & LIMITATIONS

Private by design, honestly explained.

What we do not do

  • We do not upload your files to a LifePdf server.
  • We do not create accounts or save tool history.
  • We do not include analytics, ad scripts or tracking pixels.
  • We do not store output files after you leave the page.

What to know

  • Libraries are loaded from third-party CDNs, which may receive standard connection metadata such as your IP address.
  • Selected files are processed locally, but browser extensions or a compromised device can still affect privacy.
  • Large files may run slowly or exceed your device's memory.
  • PDF “compression” is not included as a fake button: general-purpose lossless compression is not reliably possible with a simple browser-only rewrite.
